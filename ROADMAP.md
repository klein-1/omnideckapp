# Dotkit — Tool Catalogue

All 15 shipped tools below are `[LIVE]`, offline-capable, and organized into 5 clean categories.

## Categories & Live Tools (15/15)
- **Time (`time`)**:
  - `time-tally` **Tally Counter** — Multi-counter bench, custom steps, goal progress, lap log, CSV export.
  - `time-watch` **Stopwatch & Timer** — Millisecond split stopwatch & Pomodoro countdown timer.
  - `time-world` **World Clock & Date Diff** — Live global time zones, calendar date/age diff & Unix epoch converter.
- **Calc & Units (`calc`)**:
  - `calc-sci` **Scientific Calculator** — Recursive-descent expression evaluator with trig (`sin`/`cos`/`tan`), logs, powers & history tape.
  - `com-units` **Unit Converter** — Length, mass, temperature, area & regional land units, data (SI/IEC), speed & time.
  - `money-emi` **EMI, % & Bill Split** — Reducing-balance EMI, percentage suite & group tip/bill splitter.
- **Words (`words`)**:
  - `words-case` **Case Converter** — 15 editorial & developer casing styles (`AP`, `Chicago`, `camelCase`, `snake_case`, etc.).
  - `words-diff` **Word Counter & Diff** — Live metrics, keyword frequency, LCS line diff & regex replace/sort/dedupe.
  - `words-morse` **Morse & Text Codec** — Morse code (with playable audio tones), Base64, Binary, URL & ROT13.
- **Dev & Crypto (`dev`)**:
  - `dev-hash` **Hash & HMAC Generator** — 14 digests (`SHA-2/3`, `MD5`, `CRC32`, `Murmur3`), `HMAC`, `PBKDF2`, Hash ID & constant-time verify.
  - `dev-json` **JSON Formatter & JWT** — JSON pretty-printer/minifier/key-sorter/validator & local JWT claims inspector.
  - `dev-pass` **Password, UUID & QR** — `SecureRandom` password & EFF passphrase generator, batch UUID v4 & live QR matrix encoder.
- **Daily & Device (`daily`)**:
  - `ess-notes` **Quick Notes & Checklist** — Offline SQLite-persisted notes & interactive checklists.
  - `dev-color` **Color & Contrast Studio** — HEX/RGB/HSL mixer, WCAG AA/AAA contrast evaluator & 5-step shades.
  - `meas-sensor` **Torch, Level & Device** — Camera LED torch / bright screen-light fallback, live 2D accelerometer bubble level & hardware diagnostics.
